07 · How it works

Three stages, one timeline

Stated once and used everywhere, so the site cannot contradict itself.

Stage 01

Discover

We review your call patterns, lead sources, scheduling rules, business hours, escalation needs, existing software, and what your customers expect. You get a deployment plan you can challenge line by line.

Stage 02

Build & Test

We configure the voice agent, workflows, integrations, escalation rules, scripts and reporting, running alongside your existing line so nothing breaks while we tune.

Stage 03

Launch & Improve

We deploy, review performance, correct problems, and keep improving the workflows. Every deployment includes human review of early calls before it runs unattended.

08 · About

Who builds this

Nexus AI Horizon designs and deploys AI response and follow-up systems for service businesses that depend on inbound calls, lead follow-up, scheduling and dispatch.

The work is operational rather than experimental: we start from how the business already runs, connect to the tools already in use, and keep a person in the loop while the system is learning your rules.

Human oversight standard

Early calls on every deployment are reviewed by a person before the system runs unattended. Escalation rules are agreed before launch, and any call the agent cannot handle is handed to a human rather than improvised.
