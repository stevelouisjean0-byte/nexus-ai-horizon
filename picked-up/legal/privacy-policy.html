Privacy policy

Effective date: [to be set when approved]

This policy explains what Nexus AI Horizon collects when you use this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. Nexus AI Horizon is an AI automation studio in New York that designs and deploys voice, SMS, scheduling and workflow systems for service businesses.

What this policy covers

This policy covers this website and the messages we exchange with you after you contact us through it. The systems we build for our clients handle calls and messages from those clients’ own customers; that information is collected under the client’s own privacy practices, and we process it on the client’s behalf and instructions. If you have contacted a business that uses a system we built, please direct privacy requests to that business.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit the strategy call form we collect your first and last name, company, industry, work email, mobile number, preferred time to be called, and the message you write. We also record that you gave consent to be contacted by call or text, and when.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we use analytics to understand how the site is used. This may include your IP address, device and browser type, the pages you visit, and how you arrived. We use Google Analytics for this. You can opt out with Google’s browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Call and message records. If we speak with you or text you about your request, we may keep notes of the conversation and, with notice, a recording.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and set up the strategy call you asked for.
  • To communicate with you about that request by phone, text or email.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal obligations and protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Text messages

We text you only if you have consented, for example by checking the consent box on the form. Message frequency varies with your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. Our full SMS terms apply. Mobile information collected on this site is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the website and respond to you, such as hosting, email, analytics, scheduling and customer-relationship software. They may use it only to provide those services to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights or safety.

How long we keep it

We keep request information for as long as we need it to respond to you and for a reasonable period afterwards, or longer if the law requires. Our standard retention period for contact requests is [retention period to confirm].

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can withdraw consent to calls or texts at any time. Residents of some states have additional rights under their state’s privacy law; we honour those rights on request. To make a request, use the contact form on this website or write to us at the address below.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
