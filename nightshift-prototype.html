Stage 01 · Ring

Your line. Your number.

The call lands on the number already printed on your van. Nothing is ported, nothing is replaced, nothing about how you advertise changes.

Stage 02 · Answer

It picks up.

The agent answers within the response standard agreed for your account, in a voice trained on how your business talks.

Stage 03 · Understand

It works out what's wrong.

Need, location, urgency and intent are pulled from the conversation as the caller speaks — not from a phone tree.

Stage 04 · Qualify

It checks the job is yours.

The request is measured against your service area, your job types and your rules before anything is promised.

Stage 05 · Route or book

Urgent goes out. The rest gets booked.

Emergencies reach your on-call process immediately. Everything else takes an appointment slot your team can actually service.

Stage 06 · Update

It writes to your CRM.

Lead, transcript and appointment land in the system your office already works in. Nobody re-keys anything in the morning.

Stage 07 · Confirm

The customer hears back.

A text goes out with what was booked and how to change it, so nobody calls back to ask whether anyone is coming.

Stage 08 · Follow up

It keeps going, then stops.

An approved sequence runs until the customer replies, books, or opts out — and stops the moment they do.

05Solutions Four systems

One operating layer behind your phone, your inbox and your calendar.

01

AI Voice Agents

“Calls arrive after hours, during jobs, and three at once. Voicemail qualifies nobody.”

What it does
Answers inbound calls around the clock, collects what you need, qualifies the caller, books standard work, routes urgent jobs, and hands to a person when the call warrants one.
Operational result
Calls are handled the same way at 2am as at 2pm, without staffing the hours.

02

SMS Lead Follow-Up

“The lead came in Friday night. Someone will call them Monday. Probably.”

What it does
Replies to new leads by text, sends reminders, and runs an approved follow-up sequence — stopping the moment the customer replies, books or opts out.
Operational result
Every enquiry gets an answer, and follow-up happens without anyone remembering.

03

Booking & Scheduling

“Booking a job takes four messages and a person who knows the calendar.”

What it does
Lets qualified customers book themselves by voice, text or form — into slots that respect your availability, job types and travel windows.
Operational result
Appointments get made outside office hours, against a calendar the crew can actually run.

Workflow & CRM Automation

“Intake to dispatch to invoice is three people copying the same details.”

What it does
Connects intake to your approved scheduling, CRM, dispatch, notification and reporting systems, so a call becomes a record, a job and a report without a re-key.

08How it worksThree stages

Three stages. Then it just runs.

01

Discover

We review your call patterns, lead sources, scheduling rules, business hours, escalation needs, existing software and what your customers expect. You get a deployment plan you can challenge line by line.

02

Build & Test

We configure the voice agent, workflows, integrations, escalation rules, scripts and reporting — running alongside your existing line so nothing breaks while we tune.

Launch & Improve
